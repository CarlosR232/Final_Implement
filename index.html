All About Bunnies

Rabbits are small, social mammals known for their long ears, soft fur, and strong hind legs. They are intelligent and curious animals that can form close bonds with humans. Rabbits are often kept as pets and require proper care, attention, and space to live healthy, happy lives.

Rabbit Diet

The Diet of a Rabbit

A rabbit's diet should mainly consist of fresh hay, which helps with digestion and keeps their teeth healthy. Rabbits can also eat vegetables such as lettuce, spinach, cilantro, and other leafy greens. Pellets should be given in small amounts, while sugary fruits and treats should only be offered occasionally. Fresh water should always be available.
